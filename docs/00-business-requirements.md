## Product Definition

SAHL is a smart digital marketplace connecting customers with trusted service providers. Supports three service models: On-Demand Services, Contract Services, Remote Digital Services. Launches initially in one country while architected for regional expansion.

## Product Goals

**Primary**: make requesting any service easy; help providers find customers; build trust between both parties; reduce search time; simplify payments; digitize traditional service markets.

**Secondary**: increase employment opportunities; support freelancers; support SMEs; improve service quality through ratings; build a reliable digital ecosystem.

## Business Objectives

**Year One**: become the leading service marketplace inside the target country; acquire thousands of verified providers; establish customer trust; launch sustainable monetization.

**Year Two**: expand into neighboring countries; launch enterprise accounts; launch business APIs; introduce AI-powered recommendations.

## Target Users

**Customer** — a person or business looking for a service. Examples: homeowners, businesses, students, government agencies, NGOs, factories, stores.

- Goals: find providers quickly, compare offers, track requests, pay safely, receive quality work, leave reviews.
- Pain points: searching manually, no trusted providers, no transparent pricing, poor communication, no guarantee of quality.

**Service Provider** — individuals or companies offering services. Examples: electricians, plumbers, mechanics, construction companies, architects, lawyers, doctors (future), graphic designers, developers, freelancers, marketing agencies, cleaning companies, transport companies, delivery companies.

- Goals: receive more customers, increase income, manage jobs, receive payments, build reputation.
- Pain points: finding clients, advertising costs, managing appointments, payment collection, customer trust.

## Market Segments

Individual Services, Business Services, Construction, Transportation, Maintenance, Digital Services, Professional Consulting, Emergency Services, Home Services, Automotive Services, Industrial Services, Education Services, Healthcare Services (future), Government Services (future).

## Service Categorization

Each service belongs to: Primary Category, Secondary Category, Service Type, Service Duration, Location Requirement, Skill Level, Verification Level.

## Service Types

- **On-Demand**: immediate response, GPS enabled, real-time tracking, estimated arrival, normally completed within hours.
- **Scheduled Service**: booked for a future date, appointment management, calendar integration, reminders.
- **Contract Service**: large projects, tendering, milestones, contracts, project management, progress tracking, invoices, documents.
- **Remote Service**: digital delivery, file upload, chat, video meetings, milestone delivery, revision requests, final approval.

## Customer Journey

Discover → Search → Browse providers → Compare → Choose provider → Send request → Provider accepts → Payment authorization → Service execution → Completion → Review → Repeat usage.

## Provider Journey

Register → Identity verification → Create profile → Add services → Set availability → Receive requests → Accept → Complete work → Receive payment → Build reputation → Grow business.

## Core Business Features

User registration, identity verification, provider verification, location services, search engine, smart recommendations, service requests, instant booking, scheduled booking, project posting, bid management, contracts, milestones, chat, voice messages, image sharing, file sharing, video calls (future), payments, wallet, refunds, invoices, notifications, ratings, reviews, favorites, reports, support center, dispute management, referral system, promotional campaigns, advertisements, premium accounts, analytics.

## Revenue Streams

**Launch Phase**: free registration, free listings, zero or minimal commission, referral rewards, provider growth campaigns.

**Growth Phase**: transaction commissions, premium subscriptions, featured providers, sponsored services, verification fees, advertising, business plans.

**Enterprise Phase**: company dashboards, enterprise subscriptions, API licensing, marketplace integrations, analytics subscriptions, sponsored marketplaces.

## Competitive Advantages

One application for every service. Three service models. Smart provider matching. Modern UI. Fast booking. Transparent pricing. Provider verification. Secure payments. Scalable architecture. Future AI integration.

## Success Metrics

Monthly Active Users, Daily Active Users, Verified Providers, Completed Orders, Average Response Time, Customer Satisfaction, Provider Satisfaction, Average Rating, Revenue, Retention Rate, Conversion Rate, Dispute Resolution Time, Support Response Time, Payment Success Rate, Referral Growth.

## Product Principles

Simple before complex. Trust before growth. Quality before quantity. Scalable before optimized. Secure by default. Mobile first. Accessibility first. Localization first. Performance first. AI ready. Offline tolerant where possible. Future-proof architecture.

Every feature must contribute to improving trust, speed, simplicity, or business growth.
